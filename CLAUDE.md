One skill lives here: `.claude/skills/careers-check/`. When the user asks who on a list is hiring
salespeople, or gives a CSV of companies with websites, follow that skill's SKILL.md.

- Read public pages only. No logins, no LinkedIn, no personal data in outputs.
- Outputs go to `out/` (git-ignored). Never commit a user's list or results.
- Never contact anyone from this repo; it finds signals only.
